Blini

The best blini in the world!

Today we're going to make the best blini in the world! Make sure to give your family a taste!

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp melted butter

Steps:

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, milk, egg, sugar, and salt.
  2. Stir in the melted butter until well combined.
  3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and grease with butter.
  4. Pour about 1/4 cup of batter into the pan, swirling to coat the bottom.
  5. Cook for 1-2 minutes until bubbles form on the surface, then flip and cook for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown.
  6. Repeat with the remaining batter.
