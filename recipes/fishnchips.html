Fish and Chips

The best fish and chips in the world!

Today we're going to make the best fish and chips in the world! Make

Ingredients:

  • 1 lb white fish (such as cod or haddock)
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp paprika
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 lb russet potatoes
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Peel and cut the potatoes into fries. Toss them with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Spread them on a baking sheet and bake for 25-30 minutes, flipping halfway through.
  3. In a shallow dish, mix the flour, salt, pepper, and paprika.
  4. In another shallow dish, beat the egg.
  5. Dip each piece of fish into the flour mixture, then the egg, and back into the flour mixture to coat.
  6. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the fish for 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown and cooked through.
  7. Serve the fish with the baked fries and your favorite dipping sauce.
