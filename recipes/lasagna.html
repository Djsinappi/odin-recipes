Lasagna

The best lasagna in the world!

Today we're going to make the best lasagna in the world! Make sure to give your family a taste!

Ingredients:

  • Lasagna noodles
  • 1 lbs ground beef
  • 1 jar of tomato sauce
  • 1 medium onion
  • 3-4 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp pepper
  • 1 tsp oregano
  • 1 tsp basil
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 tbsp fresh parsley
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. In a large skillet, brown the ground beef and drain the excess fat.
  4. Add the onion and garlic, and cook until the onion is translucent.
  5. Stir in the tomato sauce, salt, pepper, oregano, and basil.
  6. Simmer the sauce for 15 minutes.
  7. In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the parmesan cheese.
  8. Spread a thin layer of sauce in the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish.
  9. Layer the noodles, meat sauce, and cheese mixture.
  10. Top with the remaining parmesan cheese and mozzarella cheese.
  11. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown.
